## Annual Leave Policy

All eligible full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid leave annually.

Annual leave is intended for vacations, personal commitments, family responsibilities, and other planned absences.

Employees must:
- Submit leave applications through the HRMS portal.
- Obtain approval from their reporting manager before taking leave.
- Apply at least 5 working days in advance for planned leave.
- Ensure pending responsibilities are handed over before starting leave.

Annual leave entitlement is credited at the beginning of each calendar year.

Employees joining during the year receive prorated annual leave based on their joining date.

## Casual Leave Policy

Employees receive 6 days of Casual Leave (CL) per calendar year.

Casual leave can be used for:
- Personal emergencies.
- Family commitments.
- Urgent personal work.
- Unexpected situations requiring short absence.

Employees should inform their reporting manager as early as possible.

Casual leave can generally be requested on the same day in emergency situations, subject to manager notification and approval.

Unused casual leave expires at the end of the calendar year and cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## Sick Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 6 days of paid Sick Leave (SL) annually.

Sick leave can be used when employees are unable to work because of illness, injury, or medical conditions.

Policy guidelines:
- Employees should inform their manager before the start of their shift whenever possible.
- Medical documentation may be requested for absences exceeding 2 consecutive working days.
- Employees should submit medical certificates through the HRMS portal when required.
- Sick leave cannot be used as a substitute for annual vacation leave.

Unused sick leave expires at the end of the calendar year and cannot be encashed.

## Earned Leave Policy

Employees receive 12 days of Earned Leave (EL) annually.

Earned leave is intended for planned vacations and extended personal commitments.

Guidelines:
- Employees should apply at least 7 working days in advance.
- Approval from the reporting manager is mandatory.
- A maximum of 30 earned leave days can be accumulated.
- Earned leave exceeding the accumulation limit may lapse according to company policy.
- Earned leave encashment is permitted only during eligible separation or other approved circumstances, subject to applicable law and company rules.

Employees can check their earned leave balance through the HRMS portal.

## Maternity Leave Policy

Eligible female employees are entitled to maternity benefits in accordance with applicable Indian laws.

Under the Maternity Benefit Act, eligible employees may receive up to 26 weeks of maternity leave for the first two surviving children, subject to statutory eligibility conditions.

For employees with two or more surviving children, the statutory entitlement is generally 12 weeks.

Additional provisions:
- Employees should notify HR about their expected maternity leave dates.
- Medical documentation may be required.
- Eligible employees may receive additional benefits as prescribed by applicable law.
- Adoption and commissioning mothers are covered under applicable statutory provisions.

Employees should contact HR for assistance with maternity benefit applications and documentation.

## Paternity Leave Policy

NovaRetail provides eligible male employees with 10 working days of paid paternity leave following the birth or adoption of a child.

Guidelines:
- Employees should notify HR and their reporting manager in advance whenever possible.
- Leave should generally be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.
- Supporting documentation may be requested by HR.
- Paternity leave is a company-provided benefit subject to eligibility and internal approval.

Unused paternity leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## Work From Home Policy

Eligible employees may request Work From Home (WFH) depending on their department, job responsibilities, and operational requirements.

Guidelines:
- Employees must obtain manager approval before working remotely.
- Employees must remain available during standard working hours.
- Company laptops and approved communication platforms must be used.
- Confidential company information must not be accessed through unauthorized networks or devices.
- Employees must attend scheduled virtual meetings.

Employees can request temporary WFH arrangements through the HRMS portal.

WFH approval is not automatic and depends on business requirements.

## Leave Application Procedure

All employees must submit leave applications through the NovaRetail HRMS portal.

Application process:
1. Log in to the HRMS portal using corporate credentials.
2. Navigate to the Leave Management section.
3. Select the appropriate leave category.
4. Choose the start date and end date.
5. Enter the reason for leave.
6. Attach supporting documents if required.
7. Submit the application.
8. Wait for approval from the reporting manager.

Employees will receive an email notification when their application is approved or rejected.

Employees should not consider leave approved until they receive official confirmation.

## Leave Approval Policy

Leave applications are reviewed by the employee's reporting manager.

Approval depends on:
- Available leave balance.
- Team workload.
- Project deadlines.
- Business requirements.
- Departmental staffing availability.

Managers should review normal leave requests within 2 business days.

For urgent medical or personal emergencies, employees should notify their manager immediately.

If a leave request remains pending for more than 2 business days, employees may contact HR for assistance.
