## VPN Access

Employees working outside the office must use the company VPN before accessing internal systems. Install the NovaSecure VPN client from the internal Software Portal. Sign in using your company email and password, complete MFA verification, and select the nearest corporate gateway.

If the VPN connection fails, verify your internet connection, restart the VPN client, and try another corporate gateway. Do not disable endpoint security or firewall settings to make the VPN work. If the issue continues, create an IT support ticket with a screenshot of the error.

## Password Reset Policy

Company passwords must contain at least 14 characters, including uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

Employees should use the self-service identity portal for normal password resets. After five consecutive unsuccessful login attempts, an account may be temporarily locked.

If self-service password recovery fails, contact the IT Service Desk. Employees must never share passwords, authentication tokens, or recovery codes with anyone, including IT personnel.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA is mandatory for email, VPN, cloud administration, and other company systems.

Approved authentication methods include:
- NovaAuth mobile application.
- Registered hardware security keys.
- Authenticator application approved by IT.

SMS should only be used when an approved alternative is unavailable and IT has authorized it as a temporary recovery method.

If an employee loses their registered device, they must immediately contact IT support for identity verification and MFA re-registration.

## Email Access and Troubleshooting

All employees receive a corporate email account in the format firstname.lastname@nexora.example.

Employees can access their email through the company webmail portal or approved desktop applications.

Common email issues include:
- Unable to sign in: Verify credentials and MFA.
- Emails not syncing: Check internet connectivity and application synchronization settings.
- Mailbox full: Archive old emails and delete unnecessary attachments.
- Emails going to spam: Check junk folder and report suspicious filtering behavior.

The standard mailbox storage limit is 10 GB. Employees requiring additional storage must submit an IT request with manager approval.

## Laptop and Hardware Policy

Every employee receives company-approved hardware based on their job responsibilities.

Standard laptop allocation:
- Development Team: 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD.
- Data Analytics Team: 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD.
- Management Team: 8 GB RAM, 256 GB SSD.
- Customer Support Team: 8 GB RAM, 256 GB SSD.

Employees must not install unauthorized software, modify hardware configurations, or share company devices with unauthorized individuals.

All hardware-related issues must be reported through the IT Service Desk.

## Lost or Stolen Laptop

Employees must report a lost or stolen company laptop immediately to the IT Security Team.

Required actions:
1. Contact the IT Service Desk immediately.
2. Inform your reporting manager.
3. Provide the laptop asset ID and last known location.
4. Request remote device locking and security assessment.
5. File a police complaint when required by company policy.

The IT Security Team will assess the incident, revoke affected access sessions where necessary, and initiate the company's incident response procedure.

Employees must never attempt to recover stolen devices themselves.

## Software Installation Policy

Only software approved by the IT Department may be installed on company-owned devices.

Employees can request software installation through the Internal Software Portal.

Every software request must include:
- Employee name and department.
- Software name and version.
- Business justification.
- Manager approval.
- Required installation date.

The IT Department generally processes standard software requests within two business days.

Unauthorized software installation may result in restricted device access.

## Data Security and Access Control

Company data is classified into three categories:

1. Public Data: Information approved for public distribution.
2. Internal Data: Information intended only for authorized employees.
3. Confidential Data: Sensitive business, customer, financial, and employee information.

Employees must:
- Use approved company storage systems.
- Never upload confidential data to unauthorized cloud services.
- Avoid sharing company credentials.
- Lock their computers when leaving their desks.
- Report suspected phishing emails immediately.

Access to confidential information requires appropriate departmental authorization.

## IT Support Ticket Policy

Employees must create an IT support ticket for technical issues through the Nexora Service Portal.

Every ticket should include:
- Employee ID.
- Department.
- Issue category.
- Detailed issue description.
- Screenshot or error message.
- Business impact.

Ticket priority levels:

P1 – Critical: Complete business service outage. Initial response target: 30 minutes.

P2 – High: Major functionality unavailable for multiple employees. Initial response target: 2 hours.

P3 – Medium: Individual employee unable to access a non-critical service. Initial response target: 8 business hours.

P4 – Low: General software requests, minor issues, and routine queries. Initial response target: 2 business days.

These are initial response targets, not guaranteed resolution times.

## Employee Onboarding and Offboarding

New employees receive their IT equipment and account access after HR confirms their joining details.

Onboarding includes:
- Corporate email creation.
- Employee ID generation.
- Laptop allocation.
- VPN configuration.
- MFA registration.
- Department-specific application access.

During employee offboarding, HR must notify IT at least one business day before the employee's last working day, where practicable.

IT will coordinate account deactivation, access revocation, hardware collection, and company data protection according to the approved offboarding process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. How do I reset my company password?

Answer: Visit the self-service identity portal, verify your identity, and follow the password reset instructions. Contact IT support if recovery fails.

Q2. Why can't I connect to the company VPN?

Answer: Check your internet connection, restart NovaSecure VPN, complete MFA, and select another corporate gateway. Raise a support ticket if the issue continues.

Q3. How long does software installation take?

Answer: Standard software installation requests generally take two business days after approval.

Q4. What should I do if my laptop is stolen?

Answer: Immediately report the incident to the IT Service Desk and IT Security Team. Provide your laptop asset ID and last known location.

Q5. Is MFA compulsory?

Answer: Yes. MFA is mandatory for corporate email, VPN, cloud administration, and other protected company systems.

Q6. How do I request additional mailbox storage?

Answer: Submit an IT support request with your manager's approval.

Q7. Can I install personal software on my company laptop?

Answer: No. Only IT-approved software is permitted on company-owned devices.

Q8. What is the IT support working time?

Answer: The standard IT support schedule is Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST.
